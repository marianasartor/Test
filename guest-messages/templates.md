# Guest Message Templates

Fill in the [brackets] before sending. Never share the door code until payment has cleared.

## Booking confirmation
Hi [Name], thanks for booking [Property]! Your stay runs from [check-in date] to [check-out date]. Check-in is from [time] and check-out is by [time]. We'll send your access details the day before you arrive. Let us know if you have any questions!

## Check-in instructions (day before arrival, after payment clears)
Hi [Name], we're looking forward to welcoming you tomorrow! Here's what you need:
- Address: [address]
- Door code: [code]
- Wi-Fi: [network] / [password]
- Parking: [details]
Check-in is from [time]. Message us here anytime if you need help.

## House rules reminder
Hi [Name], a few quick house rules to help you enjoy your stay:
- No parties or events
- Quiet hours from [time] to [time]
- No smoking inside
- Maximum [number] guests
Thank you!

## Check-out reminder (evening before departure)
Hi [Name], we hope you enjoyed your stay! Check-out is tomorrow by [time]. Before you leave, please:
- Put used towels in the bathroom
- Take out the trash
- Lock the door
Safe travels!

## Review request (after check-out)
Hi [Name], thank you for staying at [Property]! If you enjoyed it, a review would mean a lot to us. We'd love to host you again.

## Complaint: first response
Hi [Name], I'm sorry about [issue]. Thank you for letting us know. We're on it, and I'll update you by [time].

## Complaint: resolved
Hi [Name], [issue] has now been fixed. Thank you for your patience. Please let us know if there's anything else we can do.

## Escalation holding reply (refund, damage, safety, or review threat → goes to the CEO)
Hi [Name], thank you for raising this. I've passed it to our manager, who will get back to you by [time].
