How the 2-night total is built (estimate)

  • Cleaning fee: about £60–£120 per stay. A typical London 1-bed cleaning fee is about £85, based on May 2026 market data; the UK average is £40–£70. On a 2-night stay, cleaning adds about 15–20% to the total. (GuestReady 2026 guide, Hostfully, 5 Aug 2026)
  • Service fees: Airbnb UK guests no longer see a separate service fee, because hosts now pay a single ~15.5% fee that is built into the price. Booking.com does not normally add a guest fee either. London has no tourist tax. In practice, the total is about 2 × the nightly rate plus cleaning. (BNBCalc)
  • Data points used:
    • The area's average nightly rate was about £279 in Sep 2026, up 6% on the year. This is for BNBCalc's "Paddington – Kensington" area, which is the closest available match. (BNBCalc)
    • Kensington & Chelsea borough: average nightly rate £219 at 75% occupancy, with 1-beds at about $231. This is older data (Aug 2023 to Jul 2024). (Airbtics)
    • A listing called "South Kensington Studio" started at £130.90 a night before taxes and fees (price checked 25 Feb 2026 for a March stay). (Travelocity)
    • Another South Kensington studio is listed at £279 a night (£1,764 a week). (NY Habitat)
    • Mid-term 1-bed short lets in SW7 run about £1,200–£2,850 a week, roughly £170–£410 a night. These usually have long minimum stays. (Foxtons SW7)
    • Pass the Keys runs SW7 studios near Gloucester Road and the V&A, and UnderTheDoormat runs 1-beds near South Kensington. Neither showed a price in our sources. (Expedia: Pass the Keys, Expedia: UnderTheDoormat)

Weekday vs weekend (estimate)

We found no weekday/weekend split specific to SW7. South Kensington demand is mostly leisure (museums, Royal Albert Hall, Imperial College visitors), so we estimate Friday and Saturday nights run about 10–20% above Sunday to Thursday. That would make a Fri–Sat 2-night stay about £40–£110 more than a midweek stay. Business areas work the other way: the City of London is reported to run weekdays 20–30% higher. (STR Specialist, 2026)

Competitors

Airbnb (incl. Co-Host Network)

HIGH

Recent moves

  • 30 Sep 2026: The Fall Update adds host dynamic pricing (opt-in, rolling out in October), a 15% top-rated guest discount, an AI earnings dashboard for hosts with 5 or fewer listings, and a calendar covering multiple listings for hosts with 6 or more. AI search launches in the US first. bnbnews.gr · TechMyMoney
  • 15 Sep 2026: Remaining non-EU hosts moved to the single ~15.5% host-paid fee. EEA hosts follow on 13 Oct. The UK switch is reported as 22 Jun 2026, but sources conflict on the date. Hospitable · BNBCalc
  • Ongoing: The London Co-Host Network keeps marketing local co-hosts to owners for pricing, messaging, cleaning and maintenance. No change was dated inside the window. Airbnb London Co-Host Network

What it means for us

Free pricing tools from Airbnb plus local co-hosts make it easier for owners to manage without us. The 15% guest discount could also pull high-quality guests toward hosts who opt in.

Suggested action

Compare our AI pricing with Airbnb's new dynamic pricing on 10 of our London listings over 30 days. Turn the revenue gap into a one-page owner pitch.

Guesty

MEDIUM

Recent moves

  • 16–18 Sep 2026: The GuestyVal conference in Madrid, with 500+ property managers, focused on AI-driven automation. Guesty also appeared at SCALE Middle East on 30 Sep–1 Oct. GuestyVal Madrid · Guesty
  • Jun 2026 (context, outside window): Agent Hub launched, with coordinated AI agents for pricing, messaging, operations, finance and reviews. Travolution
  • 22 Apr 2026 (context, outside window): ReplyAI Autopilot and AI Task Creation launched, turning guest messages into cleaning and maintenance tasks automatically. Mann Report

What it means for us

London managers on Guesty, including rivals like Houst and Pass the Keys if they use it, can now match our automated messaging and cleaning dispatch. Our edge has to come from accurate scheduling and how well we execute, not from simply having AI.

Suggested action

Have ops set a target for time from a guest's issue message to an assigned cleaning or maintenance task, track it weekly, and publish it to owners.
