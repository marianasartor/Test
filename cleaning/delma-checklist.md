# Turnover Checklist (Delma)

## Booking the clean
- Book Delma for every stay, on the check-out day or on the next free day before the next check-in.
- Send her: property, date, time window, the next guest's check-in time, and any special notes.
- Get her confirmation. If there's no reply within [X] hours, follow up. If there's still no reply, alert the CEO.

## Delma's checklist
**Bedrooms**
- [ ] Change all bed linen
- [ ] Dust surfaces, check under beds and in drawers for left items

**Bathroom**
- [ ] Clean toilet, shower, sink and mirror
- [ ] Fresh towels and bath mat
- [ ] Restock toilet paper, soap and shampoo

**Kitchen**
- [ ] Wash and put away dishes; clean the hob, oven and microwave
- [ ] Empty the fridge of guest leftovers
- [ ] Restock coffee, tea, sugar, dish soap and sponges

**Whole unit**
- [ ] Vacuum and mop the floors
- [ ] Empty all bins
- [ ] Check that lights, appliances, Wi-Fi and the TV work
- [ ] Lock the windows and door

## Report back
- [ ] Photos of each room when finished
- [ ] Report any damage, missing items or maintenance needs (damage → escalate to the CEO)
- [ ] List of supplies running low
